01 / About

Who I am

I'm Elizabeth Pan — known as Eillie to the people I work and create with. Born into three languages and raised across continents, I arrived at Shanghai Jiao Tong University's Intelligent Perception Engineering program with one driving question: how do machines begin to understand the world?

I'm a second-year student who moves comfortably between the rigor of circuit theory and the ambiguity of early-stage AI research. My work at WolkenVision has given me a rare window into how large-model architectures translate from academic papers into investment-grade technical strategy.

Outside of engineering, I'm a competitive skier, a pianist who has performed for 600-person audiences, and a poetry recitation finalist. I tend to think that the same instinct that shapes a good phrase also shapes a clean architecture diagram.
